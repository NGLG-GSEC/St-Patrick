Privacy policy

We respect your privacy and process as little personal data as possible. This policy explains what happens when you visit this website, in line with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and comparable laws.

Controller

The controller is The Masonic Order of Saint Patrick, Grand Lodge. Contact details are in the Contact section.

What this website does not do

This website has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies. It does not load third-party fonts, scripts, maps or social media widgets: the fonts and the 3D viewer are served from this website itself.

Data we process

  • Server logs. When you open a page, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) receives technical data such as your IP address, browser type, date and time and the page requested. This is needed to deliver and secure the website (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). The provider handles this data under its own privacy statement and may process it outside the European Economic Area under appropriate safeguards.
  • Settings on your device. The website remembers your chosen language and, if you use the Builder tool, the content you enter. This stays in your browser and is not sent to us.
  • Messages you send us. If you contact the Order, we use your message and contact details only to reply, and keep them only as long as needed.

Names and honours of members

The website lists officers, honours and other members of the Order. Because membership of a Masonic body can reveal personal beliefs, these names are published only with the knowledge and consent of the persons concerned. Anyone listed may ask at any time for a correction or for removal.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data and do not share it, except with the hosting provider as described above or where the law requires it.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, erase or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw any consent you have given. You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country of residence. To exercise your rights, use the contact details in the Contact section.

Children

The website is not directed at children. Membership of the Order is open to adults only.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of this page.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not set cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage for two functions that you request yourself: remembering your language choice and, if you use the Builder tool, keeping the content you entered. These items are strictly necessary for those functions, stay on your device and are never used for tracking. Because of this, no cookie banner is shown.

You can delete these items at any time by clearing the site data in your browser settings.
